Solara Bay — a top-down open-world crime game in a sun-soaked coastal city of its own. You are Dee Marlowe, a driver-for-hire pulled back in by the Harbor Crew: explore the city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Solara Bay PD as your wanted level climbs, and run five story jobs — from a first fare at the Marina to the big score at Casino Solara. Traffic and pedestrians fill the streets, the minimap tracks the cops and your next job, and your cash, missions and best score are saved between visits.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Move with WASD or arrow keys, aim with the mouse, E to enter or leave a car, Space to punch (or skid while driving), click to shoot the pistol, Q to switch weapons, P to pause. Your best score and mission progress are kept in localStorage.
